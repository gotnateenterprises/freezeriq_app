Design notes

A
The attention strip is now cross-platform

One strip surfaces the top item from every system we've built: campaign health (GE-3), batches due (Kitchen Board), waiting inquiries, unpaid settlements (7E). Ranked by urgency, max 4, each deep-links to the exact board that fixes it. The dashboard's job is triage, not decoration.

was: no attention concept — 4 static stat cards
B
One toggle re-scopes everything

Today / This Week / Month drives all four pulse stats and the sparkline — the API already computes week+month with growth vs prior period; this adds "today" and binds the UI to one range state. And fundraising money finally appears on the dashboard ("Raised for groups"), fixed to the org-share framing — the current dash excludes fundraisers from every metric, which made the platform's flagship feature invisible.

was: fixed week/month mix; fundraiser $ excluded everywhere; "Family/Couple Size" hardcoded panel
C
The unified inquiry inbox with semi-auto replies

Every inbound stream in one card — fundraiser leads (GE-2), Messenger (existing meta/reply endpoint), menu-list signups, storefront questions — each with a source chip, a ✨ suggested reply built from the tenant-branded templates, and a Review & Send button. NEVER auto-sent (doctrine); the suggestion kills the blank-page problem that makes tenants reply late or never. Convert buttons launch straight into the funnel: "Start fundraiser wizard" (CRM-4) or "Add to customers." Menu signups get an automatic branded welcome (the one true auto-response — it's a receipt, not a reply).

was: Messenger-only panel; leads only in email notifications; no suggested replies; no convert action
D
The funnel is the CRM's front door

Live counts across the existing CustomerStatus pipeline; click a stage to open the CRM filtered to it. The stale-lead line ("3 silent 14+ days → send follow-ups ✨") turns the funnel from a chart into a to-do.

was: funnel visible nowhere; stage counts require visiting the CRM and counting
E
Calendar promoted from footer to co-pilot

The week strip shows the operational rhythm — cutoffs, cook days, delivery/pickup day, campaign handoffs and endings — sourced from data that already exists (order_cutoff_date, delivery days, campaign dates). The existing CalendarWidget remains the full view; this is its seven-day distillation, placed where eyes land.

was: CalendarWidget buried at the very bottom of the page
F
Fundraisers get a pulse card

Active campaigns with health + pace, plus the rebook pipeline teaser — the Growth Engine's Monday digest, live on the dashboard every day.
