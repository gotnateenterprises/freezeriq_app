Shopping & Prep Sheets

The existing Manual Planner lives here unchanged — pick bundles & quantities, generate the shopping list and prep plan, print both. Renamed so the kitchen knows what it's for, and it now pre-fills from the board's "To cook" batches instead of starting empty.

Design notes

A
Time is the first filter

The board defaults to this week — the kitchen's actual planning unit — with Today/Next week/ Everything one tap away. Today the dashboard shows every order in the system with no date lens, so a busy season buries Saturday's work under next month's.

was: no date filtering anywhere on the production dashboard
B
Four numbers answer "how's today look?"

New orders, batches to cook, boxes ready, meals due — the morning stand-up in one glance, sized to be readable from across the counter.

C
Every order appears exactly once

Today a paid storefront order shows in the Holding Area and inside the Production Queue aggregation simultaneously — two lanes claim the same work. The redesign is a strict pipeline: New → To Cook → Packed → Out. Approve moves it forward and it leaves the lane. Paid-status shown as a fact ("paid ✓"), not a separate pile.

was: production_ready orders listed in Holding Area AND aggregated in Production Queue
D
Batch cards cook the way kitchens cook

Grouped by bundle with the recipe checklist inside — "make 7 trays of pot pie filling" — and the big Pack button stays locked until every recipe is checked, so nothing ships half-cooked. Checking rows fills the progress bar; the whole card is built for a greasy thumb on a tablet. Same batch-update-by-bundle API underneath — this is presentation, not new plumbing.

was: recipe lists shown but no per-recipe check-off on the dashboard; status buttons small
E
Packed & Ready is delivery-day truth

Sorted by handoff moment (Saturday pickups, route stops, fundraiser handoffs) with labels and pickup sheets one tap away, and a single verb per card: Picked up / Delivered / Handed off. Fundraiser handoffs appear here only after closeout releases them — the boundary holds.

F
Kitchen Mode + honest naming

The header toggle scales type and touch targets ~15% for tablet use at the prep counter. "Manual Planner" becomes "Shopping & Prep Sheets" (what it actually makes), pre-filled from the board's To-Cook batches. The orphaned Schedule page gets a real tab instead of being reachable only from the Orders page.

was: "Manual Planner" label; schedule/run pages unlinked from the production dashboard
