Design notes

A
Day-first, not status-first

Delivery is a day-of-week job: the board opens on the next delivery day with its stops, pickups, and handoffs. Today's page mixes a weekly grid, inventory management, labels, and stats into one 834-line scroll; the redesign splits Supplies & Boxes and Print Center into their own tabs.

was: one page carrying inventory CRUD + orders + labels + stats simultaneously
B
Load-out math up top

Boxes left to pack, stops by zone, pickups in the window, coolers needed — the van-loading checklist as four numbers. Box/cooler counts come from the existing BoxCounter logic and supplies inventory.

C
Pack station gates the route

Each order's packing checklist (meals pulled, labels on) must complete before "Loaded in van" unlocks — and until then its route stop shows "Waiting on Pack station" with Delivered disabled. Nothing gets marked delivered that never left the building. Fundraiser handoffs appear here only after closeout releases them, staged separately with the pickup sheet.

was: run page can mark production_ready (uncooked!) orders as delivered
D
The route is the driver's phone screen

Numbered stops in delivery_sequence order (drag grips reuse the existing /api/delivery/route/reorder), one-tap Maps link, Call button, and a big green Delivered per stop with a route progress bar. The EXISTING Google routing gets top billing: ⚡ Auto-arrange calls your /api/delivery/optimize (Distance Matrix, closest-first) and 🗺 Open full route hands the driver the whole multi-stop navigation in one tap — both already built, just promoted from buried buttons to the route header. Driver Mode scales it for a phone mounted in the van. Delivered lives HERE — the production page's duplicate buttons retire.

was: two different pages could mark delivered; route page buried at /delivery/run
E
Pickups are their own lane with payment truth

Saturday-window pickups show shelf location and payment state — including "owes $45, collect on pickup" for manual orders — so whoever staffs the table needs zero context. Paid-state comes from data the orders API already returns.

F
Print Center: every paper in one row

Manifest, packing slips, batch labels, and the fundraiser pickup sheet already exist as separate pages — they become one print bar scoped to the selected day, instead of four URLs the team has to remember.
